Need for Speed: Carbon (2006) · Native for Apple Silicon Macs

Palmont,on your Mac.

No emulator. No Wine. No Windows.

Best of all worlds: The PC game on your Mac, with console-style controller layouts and button prompts, plus Xbox 360 art from your own console files.

Download Setup (1.1.0) Read the setup guide Join the Discord

You need your own copy of the game. This repository contains no game code, executables, artwork, sound, movies, tracks or textures. Setup builds the game on your Mac from your own Carbon files, and installs it alone: Carbon only. Nothing it builds is meant to be shared, and nothing is uploaded.

Better than the original.

Press F10 (Fn+F10 on a Mac keyboard, or click the touchpad on a DualSense or DualShock 4) for the settings menu. F10 > Help > Guide explains the settings.

Picture and looks

16:9 widescreen with the HUD and menus laid out for it, up to 4K, anti-aliasing. Street lamps light the road and walls at night and show in the car paint. Lacquered paint and real-looking chrome, wheels and glass. Look presets: Port, PC retail or your own picks.

Performance

Native Apple Silicon code. Shaders are prepared in the background so races don't hitch. A frame limit and an optional performance overlay.

Controllers

DualSense, DualShock 4 and other controllers macOS supports. PlayStation buttons by default on PlayStation pads and Xbox buttons on other pads. F10 > Controls > Button layout also offers PC. Rumble that follows slips, collisions, shifts and nitrous; DualSense adaptive triggers.

Sound

Surround on by default: 5.1 when your output has it, true stereo otherwise. A peak limiter stops loud moments from clipping.

Quality of life

Fixed heat level, no rubber banding, skip the intro movies or the prologue, Unlock All. Clips with sound, the last 30 seconds on a button, YouTube streaming. No EA Nation prompt at start, and updates from inside the game.

Three steps to Palmont.

  1. Download the Setup app from the latest release and open it. The first time, macOS asks you to allow it once (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway).
  2. Choose your PC game using an option below, optionally add your Xbox 360 files, and click Build.
  3. Play NFS Carbon Native from Applications.

What you can use

  • Choose your own installed Carbon PC game folder, or pick its NFSC.exe. You need the compatible version 1.4 NFSC.exe.
  • Or choose a .zip or .7z of that folder, or your original PC discs as .iso images, ISO-compatible .mdf images, or mounted/inserted discs. For PC discs, Setup also asks for the official update for your disc edition and your compatible 1.4 NFSC.exe; the disc executable is copy-protected.
  • Optional: add your own Xbox 360 files as an .iso, extracted folder, .zip or .7z for console art. They do not replace the PC game.
  • Have a .rar or .bin/.cue? Unpack it to a folder first.

Stuck? See Troubleshooting or ask on Discord.

You need an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) with macOS 14.5 or later and Apple's free Command Line Tools (Setup offers to install them).
